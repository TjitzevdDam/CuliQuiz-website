Frequently asked questions

What counts as an active player?
Someone who has genuinely played at least one session with your venue's content within your subscription period. Being invited, registering or signing in does not count. Brand campaigns do not count towards your bill either.
My team did not play for a month. What do I pay?
Only the fixed price of your package. For players beyond the included number you pay nothing if they did not play.
Do I have to write questions myself?
No, never. You pick your skill areas and the environment is filled before your team opens it for the first time. Adding your own questions and house rules is always possible, but never required.
We work with a lot of casual staff. Does that get expensive?
No, that is exactly the point: you only pay for who plays. Anyone who does not play in a given period costs nothing. For larger organisations we also agree a cap.
How does the free trial work?
Thirty days free, with no payment details upfront. Up to ten active players are included during the founding period, five after that. The trial never rolls into a subscription by itself: you decide if and when you continue.
Can I switch or stop along the way?
You can cancel monthly. Switching packages is always possible and never happens automatically: we tell you when another package is cheaper, you decide.
